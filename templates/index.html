What is Tern Radio?

Tern Radio is an experiment in breaking free from algorithmic echo chambers.

Most news apps trap you in a bubble: algorithms learn your preferences and show you more of the same, slowly pushing toward extremes and misinformation.

Tern Radio is different. We're a grassroots project that:

  • Curates news from global sources — not just your country
  • Gives you variety without algorithms deciding for you
  • Lets you choose which countries, languages, and news types to hear
  • Serves “healthy bites” — short, diverse stories you actively engage with
  • Has no ad revenue, no hidden agenda, no algorithm gaming you

How it works

  1. Select which countries and languages you want to hear from
  2. Tune your preferences — serious vs. comedy, public vs. independent voices
  3. Listen to “Just the Gist” — AI-selected key moments from each story
  4. Add stories you want to explore fully to your queue for later
  5. Build a news habit that informs without manipulating

Join the experiment. Break your echo chamber. Hear the world.
